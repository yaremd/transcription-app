A meeting with Seal

Thursday, 11:00. A pilot review with Northwind.

Follow one meeting from the moment the call starts to the moment the notes are sealed. Every screen below is the actual app.

10:59Before

Zoom turns on your microphone.

Seal notices and offers to record. A small tab waits at the edge of your screen: pause, stop, snap a slide, star a moment. Nothing joins the call.

11:00Listening

Both sides, transcribed as they speak.

Your microphone and the call's audio, on your Mac. English, Ukrainian, or both in the same call. Voices are told apart, so the notes say Dana and Marcus.

11:01A slide

Dana shares the week-two numbers.

When a new slide appears on the call, Seal reads it and keeps it beside the transcript, so the notes can use it. Video tiles are ignored. Decisions and tasks get a star on their own.

☆11:01 Decision: “Let's keep the rollout to support for now”
11:04A question

“What did we agree about the rollout?”

You missed a sentence while answering a message. Ask, and the answer comes from what was said, in seconds, without leaving the Mac. It works during the call and months later.

The answer, in seconds

“We agreed to aim for the twenty-fifth for the support rollout, with IT needing to sign off on the install by Wednesday.”

11:28Stop

You press Stop. The notes are sealed.

A summary, the decisions, next steps and action items, written on your Mac. Your own lines stay in your ink and the model's in a quieter one, so you always know who wrote what.

Sealed · 11:28 · on this Mac

Nothing left your Mac. Not a byte.
