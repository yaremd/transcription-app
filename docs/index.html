100% on-device · macOS

Meeting notes that
never leave your Mac

Seal records, transcribes, and writes up your meetings — recording, transcription, and AI notes all run locally. No cloud, no bots in your calls, no account.

Download for Mac · Apple silicon

Requires macOS 14 or later. On-device AI notes need an Apple-silicon Mac.

Everything happens on your machine

The parts other tools send to the cloud — your audio, your transcript, your notes — Seal keeps on your Mac.

🎙️

Live transcription

Hears both you and the call, and transcribes as you talk — powered by on-device Whisper.

🧠

AI notes & titles

Writes structured notes and names each meeting with a private model that runs on your Mac — no setup, no API keys.

✍️

Your jots, expanded

Type rough notes while you talk; Seal organizes and completes them from the transcript.

💬

Ask your meeting

Chat with the transcript, draft a follow-up email, or pull out action items — all locally.

🌐

Multilingual

Built for English, Ukrainian, and mixed conversations, with the right language kept throughout.

🔌

Works offline

After a one-time model download, everything works with no internet connection at all.

Private by architecture, not by promise

Seal doesn't have a server to send your meetings to. There's no account to create and nothing to opt out of. If you ever turn on a cloud model yourself, only the transcript text is sent — never your audio — and transcription always stays on your Mac.

Up and running in a minute

No bot joins your call. No dashboard to configure.

Download & open

Drag Seal to Applications and launch it.

Press ⌘N

Seal starts listening to you and the call. A small pill shows it's recording.

Get your notes

Stop when you're done — Seal titles the meeting and writes the notes.
